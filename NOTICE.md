The five complete table files are those for A028871, A028873, A201787,
A056798 and A192084. Entry snapshots support the exact definitions, baseline,
parameter conventions and attribution used in the manuscript; provider indices
are not A348425 ranks. The A201787 parameter error discussed in the paper is
in the third comment of the retained A348425 entry, not in A201787's definition.

The C/Python multiset and trial checkers were supplied during Claude-assisted
review and are preserved as executable validation code with provenance.
Original review conversations/reports and prompts are not distributed here.
Their availability does not authenticate a model version or establish provider
independence. The existing MIT notice governs the project software; upstream
OEIS material remains subject to its own notice above.

Third-party research papers and proprietary book texts are not bundled.
The manuscript's inline references give bibliographic locators; access limits
and the lack of a bibliographic-priority claim are stated in the manuscript.
No third-party license grant is invented by this package.
